Before you reserve

Common questions

How much is the reservation fee?

₱75,000 for a studio and ₱100,000 for a 1-bedroom unit, based on the official sample computations. The reservation fee is non-refundable and non-transferable, so reserve once you've chosen your unit.

Can I pay from abroad?

Yes. All transactions are processed in Philippine pesos. Foreign currency amounts are estimates only; the final exchange rate is the prevailing rate on the date of actual payment.

I'm abroad. How do I pay the monthly downpayment?

The first downpayment starts 30 days after reservation, and the developer requires post-dated checks for the first year. If you're abroad, message me and I'll walk you through the payment arrangements available to OFW buyers.

When is the turnover?

Novus is a pre-selling project with a target turnover in 2031. Turnover schedules may still change, so message me for the developer's latest schedule and I'll show you how your payments line up with it.

Who do I pay?

All payments for Novus Prime Residences are made payable to Household Development Corporation, the developer entity on the official documents.

Which view should I choose?

Each floor has 13 units facing west (UP, Villar City and the skyline) and 12 units facing east (University Town and the golf course). Prices differ slightly by floor and side, so ask me to compare the units available today.

Is the unit furnished?

Units come with the finishes listed above, including the kitchen cabinets, cooktop, rangehood, water heater and smart home devices. Furniture in the photos is for display only.
